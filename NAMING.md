# HCB naming and brand policy

## Official namespace

The official HCB repos are the ones listed in [README.md](README.md) and owned by **PerryLink** (later: the HCB organization, if created).

## Rules

1. **Do not use "HCB" to imply endorsement** by PerryLink, DeepSeek, or the HCB project.
2. Forks and derivative works **must rename** the project and remove HCB marks from the package/repo name.
3. You may say "compatible with HCB v0.x" or "consumes the HCB dataset" with a link to the canonical repo.
4. "HCB" is used as a common-law mark; it is not a registered trademark. Registration may be pursued if the project reaches a governance stage.
5. Report impersonation or confusingly similar repos to the maintainer.

## Why this exists

The name is deliberately generic ("Host Compatibility Baseline"). Generic names are easy to squat. The defense is: a canonical repo, a spec, a data license, and a public list of official repos — not a pile of empty placeholder repos.
